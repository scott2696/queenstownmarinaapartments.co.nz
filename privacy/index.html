Cookies

We use cookies for essential functions, analytics and remembering preferences. You can control or disable them at any time. Full detail — including the types we use and how to switch them off — is in our Cookie Policy.

How we use and disclose information (IPP 10–11)

We only use your personal information for the purpose we collected it for, or a directly related purpose you'd reasonably expect. We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We may share limited information with:

  • Trusted service providers who help us run the site — for example our web host, email provider and analytics provider — who process data on our instructions;
  • Authorities, if we're required to by New Zealand law or to protect our legal rights.

Some of these providers may store data on servers outside New Zealand. Where that happens, we take reasonable steps to ensure comparable privacy safeguards apply, consistent with IPP 12 on disclosing information overseas.

Storage and security (IPP 5)

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access — including HTTPS encryption in transit, restricted access to any contact-form data, and choosing reputable providers. No online service can be completely secure, but we keep information only as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected, and delete or de-identify it when it's no longer required.

Your rights: access and correction (IPP 6–7)

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you; and
  • Request a correction if it's wrong. If we don't agree to change it, you can ask us to attach a statement noting your requested correction.

To make a request, contact us via the contact page. We may need to confirm your identity first, and we'll respond as soon as we can, and in any case within the timeframe the Privacy Act requires (generally 20 working days). Access is usually free.

Accuracy and retention (IPP 8–9)

We take reasonable steps to make sure information is accurate and up to date before we use it, and we don't keep personal information for longer than we need it.

Unique identifiers (IPP 13)

We don't assign unique identifiers (such as a customer or account number) to you, because we don't run accounts. Where a third-party tool uses an identifier for its own function, it does so under its own policy.
