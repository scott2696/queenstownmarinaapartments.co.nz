What is Casino Rewards?

Casino Rewards is one of the oldest loyalty schemes in online gambling. Rather than a single casino, it's a family of 20-plus branded casinos — same trusted platform, same cashier, one shared VIP program. Play at any of them and your loyalty points and level climb together, which is the real reason players run more than one Casino Rewards account. Every member site runs on the Games Global (Microgaming) platform, so the pokies library, the live-casino floor and the banking cashier are consistent from one brand to the next; what differs is the name, the theme and the welcome hook.

The group is best known to Kiwis for its $1-deposit casinos — Zodiac and Captain Cooks — which offer a batch of chances on a Mega jackpot pokie for a single dollar. But the network is much wider than those two, spanning cheap-entry classics, table-game-leaning brands and larger multi-deposit sites like Luxury and Grand Mondial. This page maps the whole roster and explains how the shared loyalty scheme actually pays off.

How the shared VIP loyalty program works

The loyalty scheme is the reason the network exists. Join any member casino and you're automatically enrolled — there's no membership fee — and every real-money bet earns points toward one shared status that follows you across the group.

  • One VIP identity. Points earned at Zodiac count toward your status at Grand Mondial or Luxury Casino — you build a single level, not a separate one per site.
  • Two point types. You accumulate redeemable VIP points (which convert to bonus credits) and status points (which drive your tier), so casual play still nudges your level up.
  • Points convert to bonus credits at set thresholds, redeemable at any member site through your account's loyalty section.
  • Tiered levels unlock faster point earning, birthday bonuses, priority support and member-only promotions and prize draws.
  • NZD friendly — member sites accept New Zealand dollars and common Kiwi banking including POLi (deposit-only in NZ).
Kiwi tip: the group's calling card is the $1 minimum deposit. Deposit a single dollar at Zodiac or Captain Cooks and you get a big batch of chances on a Mega Moolah-style progressive — the cheapest jackpot shot in New Zealand. Because status is shared, opening a second member site doesn't split your loyalty; it compounds it. Terms change, so confirm the current offer before you sign up.

VIP tiers, points and cashback explained

Like most loyalty programs, Casino Rewards runs on a tiered structure: the more you play, the higher your level, and higher levels earn points faster and unlock better perks. Points accumulate automatically as you wager, then convert to bonus credits once you hit a redemption threshold. The scheme also layers in periodic member draws and sweepstakes tied to your status level. As with any loyalty program, points and offers carry expiry rules and wagering requirements before winnings can be withdrawn — always the detail worth reading.
