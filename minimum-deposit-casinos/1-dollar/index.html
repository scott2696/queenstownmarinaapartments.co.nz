How $1 deposits work

The mechanics are simple. You register a new account, pick a payment method that accepts a $1 minimum, and pay in your dollar. The casino instantly credits the offer — at Zodiac and Captain Cooks that's a fixed number of "chances" on a specific Mega-jackpot pokie rather than a cash match.

From there you play those chances. Anything you win becomes bonus funds governed by the offer's terms, and you clear the wagering requirement before withdrawing. Nothing about the process is different from a normal deposit — it's just that the floor is a dollar and the reward is structured as chances.

What you actually get for your dollar

The word "chances" trips people up, so let's be precise. A chance is a single low-stake bet — effectively a spin — on a progressive jackpot pokie such as a Mega slot. It is not a free-spin bonus and not a cash balance; it's a set of pre-funded plays on one specific game.

Safety, licensing and regulation at $1 casinos

A low deposit is no excuse for a missing licence.
