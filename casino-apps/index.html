How to download and install casino apps in NZ — step-by-step

There's no single "download" button for most NZ sites, so the route depends on your phone. Here's how to get a real money casino app on your home screen and start playing pokies, step by step.

iPhone / iPad (iOS)

  1. Open the casino's mobile site in Safari (a home-screen web app only works from Safari, not Chrome, on iOS).
  2. Tap the Share icon at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen and confirm. You now have a tappable icon that launches the casino full-screen.
  4. Open it, register, verify your details, make your first NZD deposit, then play.

A small number of licensed casinos also list a native app on the App Store — if one is available in your region, install it the same way you would any app.

Android

  1. If the casino is listed on Google Play, install from there — it's the cleanest route.

  4. Register, deposit in NZD, and play.
One rule that matters most: whether you take the Android APK route or the App Store, only ever install through a link on the casino's own website. A "best real money casino app NZ" download from anywhere else is the number-one way people end up with a fake.

Fake casino apps and red flags

The format isn't the danger — fakes are. A handful of scam "casino apps" slip into stores impersonating well-known brands, and dodgy APKs circulate on third-party sites. Watch for these red flags:

  • Store impersonation — an app using a real casino's name and logo but published by an unknown developer.
  • Third-party APKs — any casino APK offered somewhere other than the operator's own website.
  • Excessive permissions — an app demanding contacts, SMS or device admin access it has no reason to need.
  • No licence, no contact — no visible licence number, terms or working support.
The safe habit: always reach the casino through its own website first, then add it to your home screen or follow its own link to the store. That single step removes almost all the risk.
