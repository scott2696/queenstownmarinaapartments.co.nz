How the team works

No review is a one-person job. Hemi runs the play-testing and writes the assessment, Priya verifies the banking and payout experience, and Grace checks the compliance and responsible-gambling angle before anything goes live. Ratings are agreed by the team, not handed down by any operator — the standards behind them are set out in full on our review methodology page.

Our editorial standards

The same principles apply to everyone who writes here:

  • Named authorship. Every review carries a real byline — no anonymous, mass-produced content.
  • Real-money testing. We rate a site only after registering, depositing and cashing out with our own funds.
  • Independence. Scores, rankings and criticisms are set by the team from testing. No operator can pay to change them, and a commercial link never touches the words or the number.
  • Sourced claims. Laws, helplines and figures are linked to sources you can verify. We don't invent operator-specific bonus values or payout times.
  • Corrections. Pages show a "last updated" date, are re-checked at least quarterly, and are fixed promptly when a reader flags an error.
  • Player safety first. Responsible-gambling messaging and the Gambling Helpline appear on every page, ahead of any offer.
Note on credentials: we describe our team's real areas of experience rather than dressing it up with borrowed titles. If we cite a law, a helpline or a figure, it's linked to a source you can check yourself.
