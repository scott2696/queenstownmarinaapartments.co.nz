The benefits of using Ethereum

Ethereum (ETH) is the second-largest cryptocurrency and, for casino play, it fixes the two things Kiwis grumble about with Bitcoin: speed and cost. Here's why ETH suits regular players.

  • Fast confirmations — ETH transfers usually settle in seconds to a minute or two, so deposits and withdrawals feel near-instant.
  • Lower fees when quiet — outside peak demand, moving ETH can cost far less than a busy Bitcoin transfer.
  • Privacy at sign-up — like other crypto, many ETH sites need only an email or a connected wallet to start.
  • Provably-fair support — the same cryptographic fairness verifiers you get across crypto casinos.
  • Smart-contract payouts — some ETH-native games settle automatically on-chain, cutting the operator's review step.

Keeping Ethereum gas fees down

Ethereum's "gas" fee rises with network demand. For small, frequent deposits, low gas is where ETH beats Bitcoin.

Tax on Ethereum winnings in NZ

Same rule as all crypto here: the gambling win is tax-free, but the coin is taxable property.
