Reporting a problem with a casino

Reader reports are one of the most useful things we get, because they point us at problems we can then test ourselves. If a site has treated you unfairly, tell us what happened — the operator, the date, the method you used, and what went wrong (a stalled withdrawal, a voided bonus, a support team that went quiet). We can't act as a middleman in your dispute, but we log every report, look for patterns across players, and try to reproduce the issue in our own testing. Confirmed problems lower the site's score and get written into the review so the next Kiwi is warned. Read more about how that works on our methodology page.
