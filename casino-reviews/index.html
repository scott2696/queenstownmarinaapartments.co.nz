Trust, safety & NZ law

A casino only earns a recommendation if it's safe to play and legal for Kiwis. On safety, we check for a valid licence, an audited game platform with published RTPs, SSL encryption, KYC checks and responsible-gambling tools like deposit and session limits. On the law: the Gambling Act 2003 governs gambling provided in New Zealand and doesn't criminalise Kiwis for playing at offshore-licensed online casinos, so playing today is legal. A new domestic regime is coming under the Online Casino Gambling Act — the government plans around 15 licences with applications due 1 December 2026 — but that doesn't change the legality of playing now. On tax, recreational winnings aren't taxed in New Zealand; crypto is treated differently by IRD.
