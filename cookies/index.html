What are cookies?

A cookie is a small text file a website stores on your device when you visit. It lets the site remember things between pages and visits — like a preference you set, or that you've been here before. Cookies can't run programs or carry viruses. Similar technologies (like local storage and pixels) do comparable jobs, and where we mention "cookies" on this page we mean those too.

Third-party cookies

Some cookies are set by third parties whose tools we use — for example our analytics provider. When you follow a link from our site to a gambling operator or another external site, that site sets its own cookies under its own policy, which we don't control. If you want to know what those sites store, check their cookie and privacy policies. See also our Terms & Conditions on external links.

Managing or disabling cookies

You're always in control. You can:

  • Adjust your browser settings to block or delete cookies. Every major browser — Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox — lets you clear existing cookies and refuse new ones in its privacy or security settings.
  • Use private/incognito mode, which discards cookies when you close the window.
  • Opt out of analytics using your browser's controls or the provider's opt-out tools.
Heads up: blocking all cookies — including essential ones — may stop parts of this or other websites from working properly. Blocking only analytics and preference cookies won't break the site.

Managing cookies in your browser

Every major browser has a help page explaining how to view, block and delete cookies — look under Settings → Privacy (or Security) in Chrome, Safari, Microsoft Edge or Firefox. On mobile, the same controls live in your browser app's settings. You can usually choose to block third-party cookies only, clear cookies each time you close the browser, or wipe stored cookies on demand.

How long cookies last

Cookies are either session cookies, which are deleted when you close your browser, or persistent cookies, which stay on your device for a set period so the site remembers you on your next visit. Essential cookies are mostly session-based; analytics and preference cookies may persist for longer so we can recognise return visits in aggregate and keep your settings. You can delete either type at any time through your browser.

Consent and Do Not Track

Essential cookies are set because the site can't work without them. For analytics and preference cookies, you're in control through the browser and provider controls described above. If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" or global privacy signal, we aim to respect reasonable privacy preferences; the simplest way to opt out of non-essential cookies, though, is to block them in your browser. We don't run our own advertising-profiling cookies, so there's no ad-targeting to opt out of on this site.

How this relates to your privacy

Cookies are one of the ways we collect the analytics described in our Privacy Policy, which explains what we collect, why, how we store it, and your rights under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 to access and correct your information.
