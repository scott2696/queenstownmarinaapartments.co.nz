Who we are

PokiesRealMoney NZ is a small, independent editorial team based in New Zealand. We're recreational players and long-time followers of the local iGaming scene who got tired of "review" sites that clearly never opened an account. Every person on the team lives in Aotearoa, deposits in NZD, and knows the practical stuff — that POLi is deposit-only here, that a bank transfer can sit for days, and that a "$300 bonus" means nothing until you've read the wagering terms.

We are not a casino, a bookmaker, or an operator. We don't take bets, hold your money, or run games. We're the people who test the sites and write it down plainly. You can read about the individuals behind the reviews on our authors page.

Our mission

Our goal is simple: help Kiwis pick safer, fairer sites and avoid the ones that waste your time or lock up your withdrawals. New Zealand's online gambling market has historically been offshore and lightly regulated, which puts the burden on the player to work out who's trustworthy. We do that legwork — reading the fine print, timing real withdrawals, and flagging the tricks — so you can make an informed choice quickly.

We'd rather tell you a site is slow to pay than push you toward it. If nothing we've tested is a good fit for what you want, we'll say so.

Our editorial standards

Four principles shape everything we publish:

  • Independence. Our ratings are decided by the editorial team, not by any operator. A site can't pay for a higher score, a better ranking, or the removal of a criticism. Where a commercial link exists, it never changes the words or the number.
  • Real-money testing. We register, deposit and withdraw with our own funds at the sites we review. A rating without a real cash-out behind it isn't a rating we'll publish.
  • Quarterly re-checks. Offers, payout times and terms change. We re-check the sites we cover at least every quarter — and immediately when a site changes its terms — so what you read reflects how the site behaves now, not last year.
  • We read the T&Cs. Wagering requirements, game weighting, max-bet rules and withdrawal caps live in the fine print. We read them in full and translate them into plain English, because that's where most players get caught.

The full detail of how we score sits on our review methodology page.

How to read our reviews

Every review is built the same way so you can compare sites at a glance. Each carries a score out of 5 (our composite rating), a short verdict, a pros-and-cons box, and the key facts — banking, minimum deposit, game providers and licence. Where we mention a welcome offer, treat the specifics as a starting point and confirm the current terms on the operator's own site, because bonuses and payout processes change often.

The number itself comes from six weighted factors — payout speed, NZD and Kiwi banking, bonus honesty, game range, licensing and safety, and support and mobile — each marked from real testing. The full breakdown, including the weights, is on our review methodology page. A high score means a site tested well for us; it doesn't mean it's risk-free or the right fit for you, which is why we always point back to setting your own limits.

Accuracy and corrections

We get things wrong sometimes, and when we do we fix them. Every page shows a "last updated" date, and we re-check the sites we cover at least quarterly and immediately when an operator changes its terms. If you spot an error — a stale offer, a wrong figure, a broken banking claim — tell us through the contact page and we'll correct it and note the change. Reader reports of a casino behaving badly are some of the most useful messages we get.

Our independence

We're not a gambling operator — we're a guide. PokiesRealMoney NZ is free to read, and the part that matters to you is this: our scores, rankings and criticisms are set by the editorial team based on real testing. No operator can buy their way up the list or edit a review.

If a site is slow to pay or buries a nasty term, we'll tell you. We think that honesty is the only reason a review site is worth reading at all.
