The Legal Side of Online Casinos in New Zealand

Gambling Act 2003

New Zealand's gambling landscape is governed by the Gambling Act 2003, administered by the Department of Internal Affairs (DIA). The DIA regulates gambling within NZ borders, but the Act does not criminalise individual Kiwi players who access offshore platforms. The key principle: operating an unlicensed online casino from within New Zealand is illegal, but playing at a licensed overseas casino is not an offence for the player.

Offshore Casino Licensing

Most online casinos available to NZ players hold offshore licences (Curaçao, Anjouan, Malta and others). These provide a regulatory framework and complaints mechanism, and give Kiwi players access to thousands of games, crypto payment options and larger libraries than domestic providers offer.

Tax on Gambling Winnings

Under current IRD guidelines, recreational gambling winnings are not treated as assessable income in New Zealand — a lucky pokies or jackpot win is yours to keep tax-free. The exception is professional gamblers for whom gambling is a primary income source. See our gambling winnings tax guide.
